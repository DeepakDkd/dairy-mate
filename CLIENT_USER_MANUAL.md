# Dairy Mate User Manual

This guide is for owners, staff, buyers, and sellers who will use the Dairy Mate web app through the shared website URL.

## 1. What You Need Before Login

Please keep these details ready:

- Website URL
- Registered mobile number
- Password if you are an Owner or Staff user
- Access to the email linked to your account, because OTP is sent there
- Dairy name, if your mobile number is linked to more than one dairy

## 2. Login Process

### Owner / Staff Login

1. Open the Dairy Mate URL.
2. On the login page, select `Owner / Staff`.
3. Enter your mobile number.
4. Enter your password.
5. Click `Continue`.
6. Check your email for the OTP.
7. Enter the OTP and click `Verify OTP`.
8. You will be redirected to the portal.

### Buyer / Seller Login

1. Open the Dairy Mate URL.
2. On the login page, select `Buyer / Seller`.
3. Enter your mobile number.
4. Click `Continue`.
5. Check your email for the OTP.
6. Enter the OTP and click `Verify OTP`.
7. If asked, select your dairy.
8. Click `Login`.

## 3. First Screen After Login

Different users see different portal screens:

- Owner and Staff users open the Owner Portal.
- Buyers open the Buyer Portal.
- Sellers open the Seller Portal.

## 4. Owner Portal Overview

The Owner Portal is the main control area for managing dairies.

Main sidebar sections:

- `Portal Home` - overall owner overview
- `All Dairies` - list of all dairies linked to your account
- `Dairy Overview` - summary of one selected dairy
- `Sellers` - seller management
- `Buyers` - buyer management
- `Staff` - staff management
- `Seller Entry` - add seller milk collection entry
- `Buyer Entry` - add buyer milk supply entry
- `Profile` - your account details
- `Logout` - sign out safely

## 5. Owner Daily Workflow

For most clients, this is the easiest way to use the software each day:

1. Login to the portal.
2. Open the correct dairy from `All Dairies`.
3. Add sellers, buyers, or staff if needed.
4. Record milk entries.
5. Record buyer or seller payments.
6. Review balances and ledgers.
7. Send reminders or notifications when needed.
8. Close the month after checking entries and payments.

## 6. Managing Dairies

### View Dairies

Go to `All Dairies` to see every dairy linked to your account. Each dairy card shows:

- Dairy name
- Address
- Pricing mode
- Seller count
- Buyer count
- Staff count

Click `Open dairy` to manage a specific dairy.

### Create a New Dairy

If your account has access to the admin dashboard flow, you can create another dairy by entering:

- Dairy name
- Dairy address
- Dairy phone
- Dairy email
- Pricing mode: `FAT + LR` or `MAWA`

## 7. Seller Management

Open `Sellers` inside a dairy to manage seller records, entries, balances, and payments.

### Add a Seller

Use the `Add Seller` button and fill in:

- First name
- Last name
- Phone
- Email if available
- Dairy
- Address
- Status
- Password

### Add Seller Milk Entry

You can open seller entry from:

- `Seller Entry` in the sidebar
- `Add Milk Entry` from the Seller Dashboard

Typical steps:

1. Select the dairy.
2. Search and select the seller.
3. Fill the entry form shown on screen.
4. Click `Calculate`.
5. Review the generated receipt summary.
6. Click `Submit Entry`.

If the dairy uses `FAT + LR`, the form includes:

- Litres
- Date
- Time
- Fat %
- LR
- Milk type
- Shift

If the dairy uses `MAWA`, follow the pricing fields shown for that dairy.

### Record Seller Payment

In the Seller Dashboard:

1. Open the payment section.
2. Click `Add Payment`.
3. Select the seller.
4. Enter payment amount.
5. Select payment method: `Cash`, `UPI`, or `Bank`.
6. Add remarks if needed.
7. Click `Record Payment`.

### Seller Ledger and Reports

The seller area lets you:

- Open an individual seller ledger
- Filter by month
- Review milk entries and payments
- Export CSV reports

## 8. Buyer Management

Open `Buyers` inside a dairy to manage buyer records, milk supply, balances, and payments.

### Add a Buyer

Use the `Add Buyer` button and fill in:

- First name
- Last name
- Phone
- Email if available
- Dairy
- Address
- Status
- Password

### Add Buyer Milk Entry

You can open buyer entry from:

- `Buyer Entry` in the sidebar
- `Add Milk Entry` from the Buyer Dashboard

Typical steps:

1. Select the dairy.
2. Search and select the buyer.
3. Enter total liters.
4. Enter rate.
5. Select date, time, and shift.
6. Click `Calculate`.
7. Review the receipt summary.
8. Click `Submit Entry`.

### Record Buyer Payment

In the Buyer Dashboard:

1. Click `Add Payment`.
2. Select the buyer.
3. Enter payment amount.
4. Select payment method.
5. Add remarks if needed.
6. Save the payment.

### Buyer Ledger and Reports

The buyer area lets you:

- Open an individual buyer ledger
- Filter by month
- Review milk entries and payments
- Export CSV reports

## 9. Staff Management

Open `Staff` inside a dairy to manage employee details.

### Add Staff

Use the `Add Staff` button and fill in:

- First name
- Last name
- Phone
- Password
- Email if available
- Dairy
- Address
- Status
- Shift
- Position
- Monthly salary
- Join date
- Emergency contact
- Notes

The staff section also shows:

- Total staff
- Active staff
- Inactive staff
- Monthly salary total

## 10. Notifications

Owners can send portal notifications to buyers and sellers.

### Send a Notification

1. Open the `Notifications` panel.
2. Click `Send Notification`.
3. Select the dairy if required.
4. Choose notification type.
5. Select one or more recipients.
6. Enter title and message.
7. Click `Send Notification`.

You can send messages for:

- Payment received
- Payment sent
- Month closed
- Custom updates

## 11. Reminders

Owners can create reminders for follow-up work.

### Create a Reminder

1. Open the `Reminders` panel.
2. Click `Add Reminder`.
3. Select the dairy if required.
4. Choose reminder type.
5. Select due date.
6. Optionally link a buyer or seller.
7. Enter title and message.
8. Click `Create Reminder`.

Reminder types include:

- Buyer payment
- Seller payment
- Month close
- Custom

Reminder actions available:

- Mark as done
- Reopen
- Snooze to tomorrow
- Delete

## 12. Month Closing

Both Buyer and Seller dashboards include a `Month Status` card.

### When to Close a Month

Close the month only after:

- All milk entries are checked
- All payments are entered
- Balances are verified

### What Happens After Closing

When you click `Close Month`:

- Entries and payments for that month are locked
- The month is frozen for accounting
- You can still review data

If needed, you can use `Reopen Month` to allow edits again.

## 13. Buyer Portal

The Buyer Portal shows:

- Current balance
- Dairy contact information
- Notifications
- Monthly milk summary
- Monthly amount summary
- Monthly entry count
- Account history with month filter

Balance meaning in Buyer Portal:

- Positive balance = amount to pay
- Negative balance = advance in account
- Zero balance = settled

## 14. Seller Portal

The Seller Portal shows:

- Current balance
- Dairy contact information
- Notifications
- Monthly milk summary
- Monthly amount summary
- Monthly entry count
- Account history with month filter

Balance meaning in Seller Portal:

- Negative balance = amount to receive
- Positive balance = advance received
- Zero balance = settled

## 15. Useful Tips for Clients

- Always check the selected dairy before entering data.
- Use the month filter when searching old records.
- Use the ledger screen to verify account history for one buyer or seller.
- Export CSV when you need records outside the software.
- Close the month only after final checking.
- Keep the registered email active, because OTP login depends on it.

## 16. Troubleshooting

### OTP not received

- Check spam or junk folder
- Confirm the registered email is correct
- Try login again after a short wait

### Unable to login

- Make sure the correct role tab is selected
- Use the same mobile number registered in the system
- Owners and staff must enter the correct password

### Wrong dairy selected

- Logout
- Login again
- Choose the correct dairy when prompted

### Data not editable

- Check whether the month has already been closed
- Reopen the month if you have permission and need to correct data
